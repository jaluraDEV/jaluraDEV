### 🚀 About Me

I am a passionate game developer and systems tinkerer with a massive soft spot for the **Godot Engine**. I love building crisp character controllers, modular gameplay systems, and exploring experimental hybrid engines.

**The honest truth about my workflow:**
> *"Why spend 40 hours manually typing boilerplate when you can push autonomous AI agents to their absolute limits to do the heavy lifting for you?"*

I embrace **AI-assisted & agentic engineering** to the fullest. I view LLMs and AI agents not as simple code-completion tools, but as a relentless 24/7 engineering squad that I direct and orchestrate. I love challenging AI with complex architecture, low-level reverse engineering puzzles, and rapid prototyping — letting the machines grind out the details while I steer the ship and shape the creative vision.
